Dimo Quotations

Dimo Quotations is a Windows desktop app for small businesses. It creates business quotations, shows them exactly as they will print, and saves them as PDF files.

Your data stays with you

The app runs entirely on your own computer. Your quotations, company details and settings are stored there and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Optional Google Drive backups

If you choose to, you can connect your own Google Drive to keep backups of the app's data. The app can only see and change the backup files it creates itself, in a folder named Dimo Quotations in your Google Drive. You can disconnect at any time. The privacy policy explains this in detail.
