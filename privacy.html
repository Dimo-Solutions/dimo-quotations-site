Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Dimo Quotations desktop app ("the app") handles and how. The app is made by Elias Dimopoulos ("we").

In short

The app works on your computer. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your information. The only time the app goes online is when you choose to use Google Drive for backups, and then it only talks to Google, on your behalf.

Information stored on your computer

Your quotations, company details (such as your company name, VAT number, logo, signature and bank accounts) and settings are stored in a database on your own computer. They stay there. The app does not send them anywhere, and the app does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.

Google Drive (optional)

You can connect your own Google Account to back up and restore the app's data. You sign in on Google's own page in your web browser; the app never sees your password.

When you connect, the app asks Google for two permissions only:

  • Access to the files the app creates (the "drive.file" permission). The app uses it to create a folder named Dimo Quotations in your Google Drive, to save backup files there, to list those backup files, and to download one when you choose to restore it. The app cannot see, open or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Your email address, which the app only shows so you can see which account is connected.

While connected, the app also saves an automatic backup once a week if your data has changed, in an Automatic Backups folder inside the Dimo Quotations folder. You can switch this off in the app. To keep your Drive from filling up, the app permanently deletes the older automatic backups made by the same computer, keeping the two newest. It never deletes backups you saved yourself, or backups from other computers.

Each backup file contains a copy of the app's data, together with the date, the app version, the name of the computer that made it and a random identifier of your installation, so backups from different computers can be told apart.

To stay connected without asking you to sign in again, the app keeps a key from Google on your computer, encrypted so that only your Windows user account can read it. Your backups and this key are never sent to us or to any third party; the backups go only to your own Google Drive.

Dimo Quotations' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time (Backup screen, Disconnect). This asks Google to cancel the app's access and removes the key from your computer. You can also remove the app's access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Your backup files stay in your Google Drive until you delete them yourself. Uninstalling the app does not delete your data folder on your computer; you can delete it yourself if you wish.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not meant for children.

Changes to this policy
